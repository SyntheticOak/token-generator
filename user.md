# Custom Token Frame — Quick Guide

**What’s included**
- **Frame** — 512×512 PNG (the visible border/art)
- **Mask** — 512×512 PNG (defines where your portrait shows through)

Both files must be the same size. Use PNG with transparency where needed.

---

## How to use

1. Open the **Token Generator** editor (link from your Etsy order or shop listing).
2. In the left sidebar, under **Custom Frame**, click **Upload Frame** and choose your frame PNG.
3. Click **Upload Mask** and choose the matching mask PNG.
4. In the right sidebar, open **Portrait** → **Upload Portrait** and add your character image.
5. On the canvas: **drag** to move the portrait, **scroll** to zoom, use **rotate** controls if needed.
6. In the top toolbar, pick an **Export Size** (512 matches your frame; 1024 is higher quality) and click **Export PNG** or **Export WebP**.

**Tips**
- Upload **frame and mask** from this pack; the mask is required for correct cropping.
- Portrait can be JPG or PNG; keep files under **5 MB**.
- Your files stay in the browser — nothing is uploaded to a server unless you use the built-in portrait library.
